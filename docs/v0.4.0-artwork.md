# DiceMaster v0.4.0 artwork

Built-in image generation produced the two new texture assets under plugin/Assets/Skins. Six additional choices use runtime tinting of existing material textures, without changing those files.

## rainbow-opal.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square game dice surface texture, flat material albedo filling the entire image, orthographic straight-on, no objects or dice or numerals or text or borders. Rainbow Opal: luminous pearlescent mineral with pastel pink orange gold green cyan violet opalescent flowing patches and subtle fine crystalline veining, medium light tones, organic luxurious natural stone. Even diffuse lighting, intricate subtle texture, medium-scale pattern distributed across whole square, no central composition or dramatic shadow. This image will cover individual polygon die faces.
